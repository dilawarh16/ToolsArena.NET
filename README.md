## What Makes Us Unique

**Privacy-first architecture.** Every calculator runs entirely in your browser. Zero data leaves your device. No tracking, no servers, no storage of your inputs.

**Expert-verified formulas.** Each calculator is built and reviewed by domain experts — chemists, medical doctors, financial analysts, and mathematicians. Formulas are cross-checked against IUPAC standards, WHO guidelines, and peer-reviewed literature.

**Comprehensive educational content.** Every calculator includes detailed explanations, real-world examples, step-by-step how-to guides, reference tables, and interactive graphs — not just a formula box.

**Clean, accessible design.** Built with modern web standards, responsive layout, and accessibility in mind. No clutter, no pop-ups, no distractions.

**Free for everyone.** No accounts, no subscriptions, no hidden fees. All tools are completely free to use.
