ToolsArena provides a comprehensive library of **100+ online calculators** across multiple categories:

- **Finance** — Mortgage, loan EMI, compound interest, retirement planning, investment returns
- **Health** — BMI, BMR, calorie needs, body fat, sleep, water intake, and fitness metrics
- **Mathematics** — Percentage, linear regression, and mathematical analysis tools
- **Converters** — Length, weight, volume, temperature, speed, pressure, energy, data storage, area
- **General** — Age, days between dates, and everyday utility calculators
- **Construction** — Paint, concrete volume, and project estimation tools
- **Education** — GPA and course grade calculators
- **Much More** — Calculators are added regularly in vast range of categories useful for everyone.

## What Makes Us Unique

**Privacy-first architecture.** Every calculator runs entirely in your browser. Zero data leaves your device. No tracking, no servers, no storage of your inputs.

**Expert-verified formulas.** Each calculator is built and reviewed by domain experts — chemists, medical doctors, financial analysts, and mathematicians. Formulas are cross-checked against IUPAC standards, WHO guidelines, and peer-reviewed literature.

**Comprehensive educational content.** Every calculator includes detailed explanations, real-world examples, step-by-step how-to guides, reference tables, and interactive graphs — not just a formula box.

**Clean, accessible design.** Built with modern web standards, responsive layout, and accessibility in mind. No clutter, no pop-ups, no distractions.

**Free for everyone.** No accounts, no subscriptions, no hidden fees. All tools are completely free to use.

## Editorial Standards

All content on ToolsArena follows strict editorial guidelines:

- **Accuracy** — Every numeric value is verifiable against authoritative sources (IUPAC, WHO, NIST, peer-reviewed textbooks)
- **Originality** — Zero copied content. Every sentence is originally written for its specific topic
- **EEAT compliance** — Each piece addresses all four pillars: Experience, Expertise, Authoritativeness, Trustworthiness
- **Formatting** — Proper SI units, en dashes for parenthetical phrases, no emojis in content text
- **Value-first** — Designed to educate and inform readers while naturally linking back to the tools
